FRANCHISE AGREEMENT
This Agreement between Franchisor and Franchisee (collectively, the “Parties” and individually a “Party”) is executed and becomes effective on the Effective Date. 
RECITALS
	Marriott Companies own the System. You, Franchisee, have requested a license to use the System to operate the Hotel as a System Hotel at the Approved Location. We grant a license to you subject to the terms of this Agreement. If applicable, Guarantor will provide the Guaranty. 
Therefore, the Parties agree as follows:
LICENSE GRANT &TERM
Limited Grant. 
 Franchisor grants to Franchisee a limited, non-exclusive license to use the Licensed Marks and the System to operate the Hotel as a System Hotel solely at the Approved Location under this Agreement. 
Term.
 The term of this Agreement is stated in Exhibit A Item 4.
Reserved Rights.
 Subject to Exhibit A Item 14.C, you have no territorial rights or exclusivity. Marriott Companies reserve the right to conduct Development Activities at any location, except the Approved Location, without notice to you. You will not interfere with our Development Activities. Marriott Companies may allow other Marriott Products to use various parts of the System, including under affiliation or marketing agreements.
FRANCHISOR’S TRAINING AND PRE-OPENING TEAM
Training.
  Marriott Companies will provide training, programs, advisory and consulting services, and certain advertising, sales and marketing activities to you, similar to those offered to other similarly situated System Hotels. 
Design Review Services & Pre-Opening Training.
 One of our Affiliates, currently MIDCS, will provide design review services in accordance with Exhibit D Section 1. Our opening team will assist with the opening of the Hotel as a System Hotel, including training for Hotel employees, and the use of certain Electronic Systems. You will cooperate with us to obtain any necessary visas or permits. 
FRANCHISE FEES, OTHER FEES & CHARGES 
Initial Fee.
 Not applicable. 
Franchise Fees.
 You must pay the monthly Franchise Fee stated in Exhibit A Item 10. 
Design Review Payment.
 You must pay to one of our Affiliates, currently MIDCS, the Design Review Payment in accordance with Exhibit D Section 1.5. 
Other Fees & Charges.
 You must pay for: (i) all fees, charges and costs under the Hotel Agreements; (ii) all costs for mandatory programs for System Hotels (including the monthly ACC) and optional programs in which you participate; and (iii) any goods or services purchased, leased or licensed by you through Marriott Companies and all costs related to purchasing, installing and upgrading any Electronic Systems. The initial fee, Franchise Fee, and Design Review Payment are fixed. All other mandatory fees, charges and costs under this Agreement are computed on a fair and consistent basis among similarly situated System Hotels, and may be changed on a fair and consistent basis among similarly situated System Hotels to reflect changes in the (i) costs of providing, or the scope of, the relevant goods, programs or services; (ii) our method to determine allocation of the applicable charges; or (iii) competitive needs of the System.
Training Costs, Travel Costs & Personnel Costs.
 You will pay all Training Costs for mandatory programs and optional programs in which you participate that are not included in the ACC. You will reimburse us for all Travel Costs and Personnel Costs for individuals designated by us to provide training, services or conduct inspections or audits under the Hotel Agreements (including pre-opening training and assistance under Article 2), unless included in the ACC. Any Travel Costs will be in line with our corporate travel policies. If the Hotel is not in a sold-out position, you may be required to provide complimentary lodging in accordance with the Standards to such individuals while they are providing training, services or conducting inspections or audits at the Hotel. If such individuals are visiting more than one hotel in the market, the Travel Costs and Personnel Costs are fairly allocated between such hotels.
Timing of Payments & Performance of Services.
Timing of Payments. Franchise Fees and the ACC are calculated as of the end of each month. All payments under this Agreement are due within 15 days after date of invoice and will be made by wire transfer to our designated accounts. 
Affiliates & Designees. Any of our services or obligations may be performed by our Affiliates or designees. We may designate that payment is made to such Person, and if so, they will issue the required invoice. Any reference in this Agreement to us about payments or performance of services includes such Affiliates and designees. Any designation for the performance of services will not relieve either Party of their obligations under this Agreement.
Right of Set-Off. We may set-off or deduct any amounts owed to Marriott Companies by you or your Affiliates from amounts that would otherwise be payable to you or your Affiliates under the Hotel Agreements.
Interest on Late Payments.
 You will pay interest on any overdue amounts. Interest will accrue at the Interest Rate and is computed daily from the date such amount was due until paid. 
AFFILIATION COST CONTRIBUTION
Affiliation Cost Contribution and Programs and Services.
 We will use the ACC to fund some of the costs for certain mandatory programs and services for System Hotels (“Programs and Services”), which may include the following, to the extent applicable to similarly situated System Hotels: (i) IMF Activities; (ii) development, modification, maintenance, support, administration and operation of: (a) certain mandatory Electronic Systems, including the Reservation System; and (b) the Loyalty Program; (iii) development, operation, administration, oversight and access to certain mandatory training programs included in the ACC; and (iv) the retention or employment of personnel, consultants and other professionals to assist in the development, implementation and administration of Programs and Services, including collection and accounting of the ACC and the IMF Contribution, as well as overhead, other costs incurred in providing Programs and Services, the reimbursement of capital invested in the development of such Programs and Services, and our costs incurred to finance such capital. The ACC excludes any costs that you will pay related to purchasing, installing and upgrading any Electronic Systems, and third-party related costs, or costs for optional support services requested by you or additional support services required based on your non-compliance with Standards. The amount of the ACC contributed to the IMF is stated in Exhibit A Item 11 (the “IMF Contribution”).
Permitted Changes.
 With respect to the ACC (including the IMF Contribution), we may at any time: (i) change the method of funding (including by establishing methods of funding Programs and Services except by the ACC) and calculation; (ii) change the programs and services covered by the ACC; (iii) combine or operate the ACC and Programs and Services funds used to benefit other Marriott Products; or (iv) discontinue the use of the ACC to fund any one or all mandatory programs or services for System Hotels (v) change the local, country, regional, continental or international scope of the IMF or the IMF Activities; (vi) merge or separate the IMF with marketing funds used to benefit other Marriott Products; (vii) change the amount of the IMF; or (viii) discontinue any IMF Activities. You are bound by any such changes, which are applicable on a fair and consistent basis among similarly situated System Hotels. Such changes will not increase the amount of the ACC listed in Exhibit A Item 11 for such Programs and Services.
Benefits.
 We may use the ACC (including the IMF Contribution) to benefit System Hotels as a whole, groups of System Hotels, and other Marriott Products. We have no obligation to ensure that any particular System Hotel benefits from the ACC, Programs and Services, or IMF Activities on an equivalent basis or that the Hotel will benefit from Programs and Services or IMF Activities proportionate to your contribution.
No Fiduciary Duty.
 Marriott Companies do not hold the ACC or the IMF Contribution as a trustee or as trust funds and have no fiduciary duty to you for such funds. The ACC and IMF Contribution may be commingled with other money of Marriott Companies and used to pay all costs, including administrative costs, salaries and overhead, and collection and accounting costs, incurred by the Marriott Companies for the ACC, including the IMF Contribution. Marriott Companies may: (i) loan money for Programs and Services and IMF Activities and charge interest on any such loan; and (ii) use the ACC or IMF Contribution to repay any such loan plus interest. On request, Franchisor will provide to Franchisee a statement of operations presenting the revenues and expenses of the ACC and International Marketing Fund (which statement may be audited or unaudited in Franchisor’s sole discretion) for any fiscal year of Franchisor if such request is made between 90 and 180 days after the end of such fiscal year.
HOTEL CONVERSION, CONSTRUCTION, RENOVATION & MAINTENANCE
Number of Guestrooms.
 The Hotel will have the number of Guestrooms stated in Exhibit A Item 7. You may add Guestrooms in compliance with Sections 5 and 6 with our prior approval.
Design & Completion of the Hotel.
 You will, at your sole cost, commence the Hotel’s construction or conversion (“Construction Start”) by the Construction Start Deadline, and plan, design, construct or convert, furnish and equip the Hotel (“Design and Complete”) in accordance with this Agreement (including Exhibit D) and the Design Criteria. The Hotel must be Designed and Completed by the Opening Deadline. 
Maintenance & Quality Assurance Program.
 You will, at your sole cost, maintain and make repairs, alterations, and replacements to the Hotel in conformity with Laws and the Standards. You will not undertake any material alterations to the Hotel without our prior consent, unless required by Laws or if needed for the continued safe and orderly operation of the Hotel.
Periodic Renovations & Review of Plans.
Replacement of Soft Goods and Case Goods. You will periodically renovate all Guestrooms and Public Facilities in accordance with the Standards, including: (i) a Soft Goods renovation at least once every seven years after the date such Soft Goods were installed; and (ii) a Case Goods renovation at least once every 14 years after the date such Case Goods were installed; however, we may require earlier, or permit later, renovations or replacements depending on the condition of the Hotel.
Material Renovations Review. At least 60 days before beginning any periodic renovation under Section 5.4.A or a material renovation of the Hotel, you will provide Plans incorporating the Standards for renovation for our review. We will review the Plans only for compliance with the Standards and not for compliance with Laws. If we determine that the Plans do not comply with the Standards, we may require changes, and you will deliver revised Plans incorporating the changes. You will (i) prepare color boards, drawings and furnished models of the Guestrooms for our acceptance before purchasing FF&E for any renovation, and (ii) not begin renovation until we accept the Plans and the proposed FF&E. You will promptly deliver to us the final Plans, which will not be changed without our prior consent. We may charge a fee based on our standard hourly rates for review of the Plans and related Travel Costs.
Compliance with Laws & Funding. You will ensure that any renovations comply with Laws and the Standards, including any accessibility requirements. We will have no liability or obligation concerning the means, methods or techniques used in renovating the Hotel. You will not reproduce, use or permit the use of any Standards, except for the Hotel. You will fund the cost of all renovations and replacements of FF&E.
Design & Independent Hotel Brand.
 The Standards do not specify the Design or Independent Hotel Brand. You will: (i) create and maintain an identifiable Design and Independent Hotel Brand for the Hotel, subject to MIDCS’ acceptance; and (ii) if we require, retain a branding consultant and an interior design firm acceptable to us to develop a communications and marketing plan, implement a service experience program, and create spaces and experiences to reinforce the Design and Independent Hotel Brand. Nothing in this Section will: (x) affect your obligation to maintain the Hotel in compliance with Section 5.3, or (y) limit our right to approve or set Standards for the quality or condition of the Hotel. 
FURNITURE, FIXTURES, EQUIPMENT, INVENTORIES & SUPPLIES
Uniformity of System.
 You will only use FF&E, Inventories and Fixed Asset Supplies that comply with the Standards to maintain the identity, integrity and reputation of the System. We may designate suppliers (including us for (iii) and (iv)) for items related to: (i) life safety; (ii) signage; (iii) Electronic Systems; and (iv) Intellectual Property. You may otherwise purchase FF&E (other than designated Electronic Systems including Opportunity Management Systems, Reservation System, Yield Management System, Mobile Device and Application Management and Mobile Application Access (Mobile Device and Application Management and Mobile Application Access being an optional item), Continent IT Services, Intranet Website, and MDash, of which their respective supply price is set out in the Disclosure Document provided to Franchisee and is subject to change), Inventories and Fixed Asset Supplies from any supplier that complies with the Standards and Laws. You may be permitted or required to participate in arrangements with Travel Management Companies and other booking agents negotiated by us, and will pay for such arrangements in a timely manner failing which we may suspend or terminate your participation in such arrangements. Any such arrangements are made available or required on a fair and consistent basis with similarly situated System Hotels. 
Suppliers.
 If we designate a supplier for an item under Section 6.1, you may propose alternative suppliers by delivering sufficient information and samples for us to confirm that such item meets the Standards, and the proposed supplier can provide such item to the Standards. We may require (i) reimbursement for the cost of such review, and (ii) that suppliers of certain designated items have insurance protecting the Parties. We may refuse to permit future purchases if the supplier fails to meet the requirements of this Section or the Standards.

Franchisee’s Local Advertising & Marketing Programs.
Local Advertising. You will undertake local advertising, marketing, promotional, sales and public relations programs and activities for the Hotel, including preparing and using any Marketing Materials, in accordance with the Standards and Laws.
Use of Signs, Marketing Materials & Proprietary Marks. You will only use signs and other Marketing Materials and the Proprietary Marks in the places, combination, arrangement and manner we approved or required and in accordance with the Standards and Laws. We will take into consideration any contracts you have and the amount of your existing supply of such Marketing Materials. International Marketing Fund.

The IMF Activities may be conducted on a local, country, regional, continental, international or Category basis.
Additional Sales & Marketing Programs.
 You (i) will participate in Additional Sales and Marketing Programs that are mandatory for similarly situated System Hotels, and (ii) may participate in optional ones.
7.4. 	Pricing, Rates & Reservations.
Pricing & Rates. You are responsible for setting your own prices and rates for Guestrooms and other products and services at the Hotel, including determining any prices or rates that appear in the Reservation System. We may, however: (i) prohibit certain types of charges or billing practices that we determine are misleading or detrimental to the System, including price-gouging or incremental fees for services that guests would normally expect to be included in the Guestroom charge; (ii) require you to price consistently in all distribution channels; or (iii) impose other pricing requirements permitted or required by Laws.
Honoring Reservations. You will (i) provide your prices and rates for use in the Reservation System in accordance with the Standards, (ii) honor any prices, rates, or discounts that appear in the Reservation System, or any other publication, program, or promotion; (ii) honor all reservations made through the Reservation System or that are confirmed; and (iii) not charge any Hotel guest a rate higher than the rate specified for the Hotel guest’s reservation in the Reservation System or the reservation confirmation. You will also honor all pricing and terms for any other product or service offered in connection with the Hotel.
Pricing Recommendations; Participation in Programs. We may recommend prices or rates for the products and services you offer or require participation in various sales or Inventory Management programs or promotions we offer, to the extent permitted under Laws. Our recommendations are not mandatory nor a representation or warranty that the use of such recommended prices or rates will produce, increase, or optimize your profits. You are ultimately responsible for determining and making decisions on the prices or rates at which you offer Hotel related products and services. We will have no liability for any such recommendations, including those made in connection with any sales activity or Inventory Management program. We may provide Inventory Management or sales services at the request of, and as Sales Agent for, you. If we are acting as your Sales Agent, you consign Hotel inventory to us, and you retain all risk of loss of inventory that goes unsold or is sold at a reduced price.

 At your cost, you will: (i) obtain, install, maintain, use and replace at the Hotel all Electronic Systems in compliance with the Standards; and (ii) take any other actions required by the Standards to protect the Electronic Systems and the data stored or communicated via the Electronic Systems. HOTEL OPERATIONS
Opening & Operating Hotel.
 Hotel Operator will at all times operate the Hotel as a System Hotel in compliance with the Standards and this Agreement.
Required and Prohibited Activities. You will: (i) participate in all mandatory programs and offer all guest services required for System Hotels; (ii) provide all food and beverage service in accordance with the Standards and Laws; and (iii) make all Hotel-related payments in accordance with the terms of all contracts and invoices. Except as permitted in the Standards or with our prior consent, you will not (a) knowingly permit adult entertainment activities or gambling or associate the Hotel with any casino or gaming activities; or (b) operate any nightclub at the Hotel.
Inspection Rights. We may inspect the Hotel during business hours to confirm that you comply with this Agreement and the Standards, and to test the Hotel’s equipment, food products, and supplies. You will pay any costs related to such inspections, including on site costs of third-party inspectors, to the extent not covered by the ACC. 
Standards & System Noncompliance. If you fail to achieve the Quality Assurance Program threshold scores, Standards audits or to comply with the Standards or other aspects of the System, we may, at your cost: (i) require your and your personnel to attend or conduct training, (ii) provide assistance at the Hotel to remedy such non-compliance, and (iii) conduct additional inspections and audits. 
System Promotion & No Diversion to Other Businesses.
 You will refer reservation requests that cannot be fulfilled by the Hotel to other System Hotels or other Marriott Products in accordance with the Standards. You will not (i) use the Hotel for any business except operating a System Hotel, or (ii) use any part of the Hotel or the System to promote or divert business to any business except the Hotel, including the advertising or promotion of hotels, vacation or timeshare facilities, or any similar product sold on a periodic basis (including those which you or your Affiliates operate or have an Ownership Interest in) not operated under a trade name or trademark owned by Marriott Companies. You will not offer complimentary or reduced-price Guestrooms to benefit any business except the Hotel.
Employees.
Hotel Staffing and Employment Matters. You will ensure that (i) Hotel employees are suitably qualified and comply with the Standards, including completing all required trainings; and (ii) the Hotel is sufficiently staffed. The Hotel’s general manager will devote full time to the management of the Hotel and supervision of employees. All employment decisions at the Hotel are made solely by the Hotel Operator. We do not direct or control the employment policies or decisions for the Hotel. All employees at the Hotel are solely Hotel Operator’s and not our employees. The Hotel Operator is not our agent for any purpose regarding Hotel employees. 
Communication. We may communicate directly with the Hotel’s managers and Hotel Operator about the Hotel’s day-to-day operations and rely on their statements. We will under no circumstances direct or control the Hotel operations.
Operator of Hotel.
Franchisor Consent Required. The Hotel will only be operated by a Person consented to in advance by us. Such Person can either be you or a management company (in each case the “Hotel Operator”). If you retain a management company, you remain responsible for complying with the obligations of this Agreement and in case of any conflict between this Agreement and your agreement with the management company, this Agreement will control. 
Consent and Conditions. We may withhold our consent to a Hotel Operator that we determine (i) is not financially capable, does not have the managerial skills or operational capacity required to operate the Hotel in accordance with the Standards and this Agreement; (ii) is not a Qualified Person; or (iii) does not provide us with all information we reasonably request. We may condition our consent to any proposed Hotel Operator, including requiring participation in one or more programs. You will execute such documents as we require to effectuate participation in such programs and pay the cost of any such programs. 
Change in Circumstances. You will promptly notify us if you become aware (i) of a change in Control of the Hotel Operator; or (ii) if any of the requirements in Section 9.4 B, the Hotel Operator Consent, or the Standards are no longer met by the approved Hotel Operator. In such case we may withdraw our consent at any time. We may require you to (a) terminate your agreement with the management company, if applicable, and (b) provide a replacement Hotel Operator, which is subject to our consent. If any amendment to this Agreement is required in connection with a Hotel Operator change, you will pay our outside counsel costs related to such amendment. 
DATA PROTECTION
Controllership.
 The Parties agree that Franchisee, Marriott International, Inc., and Franchisor are each independent controllers of Guest Personal Data and may share Guest Personal Data during the Term to the extent permitted by Laws and the Standards. Your use of Guest Personal Data is subject to Section 12.5, and such Guest Personal Data is collected, used and shared by you only for the benefit of the Hotel and in accordance with this Agreement, the Standards and Laws.
Signing of Documents. 
 The Parties will take such actions and sign such documents that we determine are necessary to enable each Party to comply with Laws applicable to Guest Personal Data related to the Hotel, such as data transfer agreements. 
Notification Requirements.
 You will promptly inform us if you: (i) discover or reasonably suspect a Security Incident; (ii) have been contacted by any Person seeking to exercise any individual rights under Laws regarding Guest Personal Data; or (iii) have been contacted by a data protection authority about processing of Guest Personal Data (in which case we may control any proceedings with such authority). The Parties will cooperate with the other as is reasonably necessary (a) to respond to data access requests related to Guest Personal Data; and (b) in the resolution of Security Incidents at the Hotel or by you. 
STANDARDS & SYSTEM
You agree that conformity with the Standards and System is essential to maintain the uniformity and quality of guest service of System Hotels. You will comply with the then-current Standards and operate the Hotel in compliance with the System and the Hotel Agreements. Marriott Companies may modify the Standards and System, including changing, adding, or deleting elements of the Standards or System. Modifications to the System may be made for all System Hotels or for any Category of System Hotels on a fair and consistent basis. We may allocate the costs of System modifications among System Hotels or any Category of System Hotels on a fair and consistent basis.
