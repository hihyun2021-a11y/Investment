The Hyoosik, a company organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of Korea with its registered office at 2nd floor, Youngdong Daero 76th street 12, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Korea (“Franchisee”); and

KORAMCO LIFE INFRA, a company organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of Korea with its registered office at 511, Samseong-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, Korea (“Owner”).

RECITALS

A.	Franchisor and Franchisee are parties to a franchise agreement dated as of February 26, 2026 (as such agreement may be amended, the “Franchise Agreement”) relating to the Hotel, which is currently known as Anook Hotel Incheon Guwol. 
B.	Franchisee and Owner have entered into a lease (the “Lease”) under which Owner has granted Franchisee possession and Control of the Hotel. 
C.	Owner, Franchisee and Franchisor want the Hotel to be operated as a System Hotel under the terms of the Franchise Agreement and this Agreement.
NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Owner, Franchisee and Franchisor agree as follows:
Definitions.  Owner confirms that it has received a copy of the Franchise Agreement and is aware of its content. Capitalized terms used but not defined herein are defined in the Franchise Agreement.

Acknowledgment of Lease. Owner represents and warrants that it has entrusted the Hotel to the Trustee (as defined in Section 6) under the Trust Deed (as defined in Section 6) and the Trustee is the sole owner of the Hotel. Owner acknowledges and agrees that throughout the Term of the Franchise Agreement: (i) Franchisee is bound by the Hotel Agreements in accordance with the terms thereof; (ii) under the Lease, Franchisee (as lessee) has exclusive possession and Control of the Hotel, and the right to Design and Complete, renovate and manage the Hotel in accordance with the Franchise Agreement; and (iii) each of Owner and Franchisee will comply with all their respective obligations under the Lease and not amend, modify or waive any of the provisions in the Lease that materially and adversely restricts, limits or interferes with the rights of Franchisor or its Affiliates under this Agreement or any of the Hotel Agreements, without the prior written consent of Franchisor. Owner will copy Franchisor on any notice of default to Franchisee under the Lease.

Term of the Lease; Non-Disturbance.  Owner will ensure that: (i) the term of the Lease is renewed or extended for a period at least as long as the Term of the Franchise Agreement; (ii) none of the Hotel Agreements will be terminated and none of Franchisor or its Affiliates will be disturbed in their rights under the Hotel Agreements during the Term of the Franchise Agreement, even if any legal action is taken under the Lease or if the Lease is terminated, surrendered or expires (a “Lease Termination”); (iii) any successor owner will take the Hotel subject to the Lease and the Hotel Agreements; and (v) any Transfer by Owner or any successor owner will be subject to the requirements under Section 15 of the Franchise Agreement and the successor owner will assume in writing all relevant rights and obligations of Owner under this Agreement. 

Lease Termination. 

A.	If a Lease Termination occurs, Owner will either:

cause a “Substitute Franchisee” (as defined below), from the Lease Termination date: (a) to assume Franchisee’s rights and obligations under the Lease, the Hotel Agreements and this Agreement, and the Lease will immediately be deemed to be novated as between Franchisee, Substitute Franchisee and Owner, and the Hotel Agreements and this Agreement will immediately be deemed to be novated as between Franchisee, Substitute Franchisee, Owner, Franchisor and its Affiliates; or (b) to enter into a new lease with Owner in substantially the same form as the Lease for a term equal to or longer than the term of the Franchise Agreement (“New Lease”), and assume the rights and obligations of the Franchisee under the Hotel Agreements and this Agreement, whereupon the Hotel Agreements and this Agreement will immediately, upon the execution of such New Lease, be deemed to be novated as between Franchisee, Substitute Franchisee, Owner, Franchisor and its Affiliates; or

	(ii) 	if Owner satisfies the requirements of a Substitute Franchisee under Section 4.B below, directly assume all of Franchisee’s rights, benefits, obligations and liabilities under the Hotel Agreements with respect to the period(s) after the Lease Termination, and the Hotel Agreements will immediately be deemed to be novated as between Franchisee, Owner, Franchisor and its Affiliates.

B.	A proposed substitute franchisee must meet all of the following conditions and be approved by Franchisor in its sole discretion (“Substitute Franchisee”):  (i) be a Qualified Person; and (ii) as determined by Franchisor, be: (x) financially capable of performing its obligations under the Franchise Agreement; (y) qualified in managerial and operational capacity to operate the Hotel (unless a third party management company consented to by Franchisor will operate the Hotel); and (z) able to perform the obligations under the Franchise Agreement. Owner will provide Franchisor with the identity of the proposed Substitute Franchisee and its Interestholders, and such other documents and information reasonably requested by Franchisor to consent to Substitute Franchisee and execute any related documents acceptable to Franchisor for the Hotel Agreements to be novated as between Franchisee, Substitute Franchisee, Owner, Franchisor and its Affiliates. Owner will pay Franchisor’s reasonable outside counsel costs, if any, related to the Lease Termination and its documentation.

Default under any Hotel Agreement. Franchisor will copy Owner on any notice of default issued to Franchisee under any Hotel Agreement. If not cured by Franchisee, Owner will have the right (but not obligation) to cure such default on behalf of Franchisee during the cure period stated in the default notice.

Trust. As at the Effective Date and throughout the Term, Owner represents, covenants and warrants to Franchisor the following: (i) Owner has been duly incorporated and is validly existing under the laws of the Republic of Korea; (ii) Owner has full and valid power and authority as the trustor and beneficial owner of the entrusted Hotel, to enter into this Agreement and to carry out the transactions contemplated by this Agreement; (iii) Owner has in full force and effect the authorizations necessary for Owner to enter into this Agreement and perform its obligations under them and allow them to be enforced; (iv) Owner has duly obtained or given all consents and approvals of a third party or notice to a third party necessary in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement; (v) Owner has entrusted the Hotel to Kyobo Asset Trust Co., Ltd. (the “Trustee”) under a real estate collateral trust agreement (the “Trust Deed”) and no action has been taken or proposed to be taken to remove the Trustee as trustee of the entrusted Hotel; (vi) Owner has obtained all authorizations necessary for Owner to enter into the Trust Deed and the Lease and perform its obligations under them and allow them to be enforced; (vii) Owner has duly obtained or given all consents and approvals of a third party or notice to a third party necessary in connection with the execution, delivery and performance of the Lease, including, as applicable, the prior written consent of the Trustee and the preferred beneficiaries of the trust, in each case except to the extent such consent or notice is not required under the Trust Deed; (viii) Owner is not, and has never been, in default under the terms of the Trust Deed or the Lease; (ix) the Trust Deed and the Lease provided to Franchisor prior to the Effective Date is accurate and complete as at the Effective Date, and neither has been varied, amended or supplemented in any way; (x) the Trust Deed and the Lease will not be amended in any manner which will materially and adversely affect either Franchisee’s ability to operate the Hotel pursuant to the Hotel Agreements and/or Franchisor’s rights under the Hotel Agreements.

Miscellaneous. 

The term of this Agreement will commence on the date set forth above and will run concurrently with the Term of the Franchise Agreement. In addition, this Agreement will terminate concurrently with the termination of the Franchise Agreement. 

All notices and other communications under this Agreement must be in writing, and must be sent or delivered by the methods and to the addresses for Franchisor and Franchisee as required under the Franchise Agreement.  The address for Owner for purposes of such notices is set out in Exhibit A.
		
This Agreement will bind and inure to the benefit of Franchisor and its Affiliates under the Hotel Agreements, and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

Owner, Franchisee and Franchisor agree that Section 12.6 (Confidentiality of Terms), Section 23 (Governing Law; Arbitration & Related Issues), Section 15.3 (Franchisor Transfers), Section 19 (Condemnation & Casualty), Section 26.2 (Counterparts) and Section 26.3 (Construction & Interpretation) of the Franchise Agreement are incorporated herein by reference and are amended only as necessary to apply to this Agreement. This Section 7.D survives termination or expiration of the Franchise Agreement.
